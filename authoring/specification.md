# Corpus authoring specification

The wiki is fictional, English-language, and independent of SinapsiDB. Write substantive documents as employees would use them: policies, procedures, technical specifications, troubleshooting notes, meeting records, decisions, product documentation and archived revisions. Use portable relative Markdown links.

Keep stable identifiers for entities, processes, rules, incidents and systems. Record owners, effective dates and supersession relationships where appropriate. Treat the company profile and organization baseline as canonical until a documented change is introduced. Do not invent new sites or departments without updating the baseline.

Maintain realistic dependencies between business and technical documents. Distinguish authoritative policy, implementation behavior and informal notes. Intentional contradictions must have a plausible historical or operational explanation, and their resolution must be discoverable in evidence when the benchmark expects a definitive answer.

Target 3,000 substantive pages, not mechanically duplicated templates. Expand by domain in reviewed batches; validate relative links, unique IDs, headcount totals, effective dates and cross-domain facts. Keep benchmark questions, gold answers, generation prompts and validation data outside wiki/ to avoid contaminating retrieval experiments. The reference date is 2026-11-26. No real employee or confidential corporate data may be used.
