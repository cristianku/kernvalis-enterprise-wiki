Engineering component master. Function: motion or sensor control. Critical characteristic classification: critical. Reference unit cost for planning: USD 102. Preferred sourcing reference: [SUP-0003](../suppliers/SUP-0003.md); a preferred source is not proof of current qualification or approved ordering authority.

## Acceptance and configuration

Incoming verification: functional electrical test. Material identity and lot must remain traceable through production. The component may be used only where listed in the effective product BOM. An alternate requires controlled substitution approval, even if commercial specifications appear equivalent.

For critical components, release requires the appropriate inspection evidence. For standard components, the receiving procedure still requires identity and quantity verification.
