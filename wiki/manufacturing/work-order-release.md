# Work order release (MFG-02)

Owner: Manufacturing and Quality. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Production requires approved BOM revision, released components, qualified operators and usable assets. A pending engineering change does not authorize early revision use.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
