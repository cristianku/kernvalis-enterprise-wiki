# Production shift handover (MFG-03)

Owner: Manufacturing. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Outgoing shifts record work order status, quantity, holds, abnormal equipment conditions and unresolved safety issues. Incoming staff acknowledge handover before continuation. Do not restart a stopped asset without release evidence.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

An open maintenance restriction must be carried into the next shift rather than cleared by a schedule change.
