# Preventive maintenance (MFG-01)

Owner: Manufacturing. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Safety-critical assets require preventive maintenance every 90 calendar days; standard assets every 180 days. A corrective repair does not reset the interval unless the full preventive checklist was completed. Overdue safety-critical assets must be removed from operation.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
