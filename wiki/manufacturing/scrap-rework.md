# Scrap and rework accounting (MFG-04)

Owner: Manufacturing and Quality. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Record good units, scrap, rework and remaining work in process separately. Scrapping requires disposition and quantity evidence. Reworked units are counted as good only after successful reinspection. Yield is accepted good quantity divided by processed quantity.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A unit awaiting reinspection must not be reported as accepted output.
