# Returns (SVC-03)

Owner: Customer Service and Quality. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Returns require RMA identifier, serial and classification. Safety returns must be quarantined. Three distinct serials with the same confirmed manufacturing defect within 30 days trigger a quality investigation. RMA does not imply warranty or refund approval.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
