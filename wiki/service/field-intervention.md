# Field service intervention (SVC-04)

Owner: Customer Service. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Field service requires a defined equipment identifier, approved scope, safe access arrangements and technician qualification. Record configuration before and after intervention. Safety findings require escalation to Quality.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

An undocumented replacement prevents reliable traceability and cannot close a safety case.
