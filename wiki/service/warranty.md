# Product warranty (SVC-01)

Owner: Customer Service. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Standard warranty is 24 months from shipment, unless an extended contract applies. Normal wear, misuse and unauthorized modifications are excluded unless explicitly overridden. Manufacturing date is not the coverage start date.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
