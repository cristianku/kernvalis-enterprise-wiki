# Service escalation (SVC-02)

Owner: Customer Service. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

P1: complete production stoppage or immediate safety impact; acknowledge within one elapsed hour. P2: severe degradation; acknowledge within four elapsed hours. P3: routine; acknowledge within 24 elapsed hours. Use opening to first human response in UTC.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
