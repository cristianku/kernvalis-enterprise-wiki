# Foreign-currency transaction accounting (FIN-10)

Owner: Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Record original transaction currency and the approved USD equivalent at recognition. Use the month-end closing rate for open monetary balances. Realized and unrealized exchange differences must be distinguished. The rates in approved historical records are authoritative for this synthetic company.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A customer credit check uses USD equivalent exposure, not a sum of unconverted currencies.
