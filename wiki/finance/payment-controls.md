# Vendor payment authorization (FIN-11)

Owner: Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Payment requires an approved invoice and matched receipt or explicitly approved service evidence. Vendor bank changes require independent verification through a previously established contact channel. The person changing payment details cannot release the payment.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

An email requesting a new bank account is not sufficient evidence of a valid change.
