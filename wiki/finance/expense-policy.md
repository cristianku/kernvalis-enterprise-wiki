# Expense reimbursement (FIN-03)

Owner: Finance. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Expenses above USD 75 require itemized receipts; hotel expenses always require receipts. Claims must be filed within 60 calendar days. Direct managers approve ordinary claims and Finance approves exceptions. No self-approval.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
