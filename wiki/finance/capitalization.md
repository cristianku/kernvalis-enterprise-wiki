# Capitalization of equipment and projects (FIN-05)

Owner: Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Tangible equipment with cost at least USD 5,000 and useful life beyond one year is capitalized when available for intended use. Training, routine maintenance and preliminary feasibility studies are expensed. Project authorization does not determine accounting treatment; Controller approval of the asset register is separate.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A USD 40,000 assembly fixture is capitalized when operational; operator training is expensed.
