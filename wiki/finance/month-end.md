# Month-end close (FIN-04)

Owner: Finance. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Reconciliation is due by the third business day after month-end. Unresolved differences above USD 5,000 must be escalated before close. Backdated postings after close need controller approval.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
