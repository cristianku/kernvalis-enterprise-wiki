# Customer credits and refunds (FIN-09)

Owner: Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Credit notes require a validated invoice reference and reason code. Above USD 10,000, Controller approval is required. Cash refunds require a separate payment authorization and verified recipient details. A warranty replacement does not imply a cash refund.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A return authorization permits shipment back but does not justify crediting an invoice without a financial decision.
