# Intercompany reconciliation (FIN-06)

Owner: Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Intercompany receivable and payable entries must carry the same transaction identifier, trading counterpart and currency reference. Differences are investigated before consolidation. Do not offset external customer debt against unrelated intercompany balances.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

An invoice posted by Dallas without a corresponding Monterrey payable requires reconciliation.
