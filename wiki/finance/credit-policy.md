# Commercial credit policy (FIN-CR-01)

Owner: Corporate Finance. Effective 2025-07-01. Currency: USD equivalent.

Credit exposure comprises unpaid invoices, unbilled shipments and open order value, excluding the candidate order until evaluated. The candidate order is approved automatically only if resulting exposure does not exceed the customer credit limit and no undisputed invoice is more than 30 calendar days overdue.

Finance may approve a temporary limit increase of up to USD 50,000 with a documented expiry date not exceeding 30 calendar days. Larger increases require CFO approval. Prepaid orders may bypass exposure checks only after cleared funds are recorded; overdue debt requires separate Finance clearance.

Example: a USD 100,000 limit, USD 80,000 existing exposure and USD 25,000 candidate order requires an exception even if stock is available.
