# Budget and forecast control (FIN-07)

Owner: Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

The fiscal year closes December 31. An approved annual budget authorizes planning limits, not individual purchase commitments. Monthly forecasts update expected spend but do not revise approved budget unless a controlled transfer is approved. Variances above USD 50,000 or 10% of departmental monthly budget, whichever is lower, require commentary.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A department with a USD 200,000 monthly budget and USD 25,000 overspend must explain the variance.
