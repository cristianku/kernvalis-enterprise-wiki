# Travel authorization (HR-02)

Owner: Human Resources and Finance. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

International travel needs manager approval and destination risk assessment. Premium cabin requires Finance approval unless documented accommodation applies.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
