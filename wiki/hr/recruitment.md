# Recruitment and hiring (HR-03)

Owner: Human Resources. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Hiring requires approved headcount, role description, documented selection and employment authorization. An accepted offer is not active employment before the effective start date. The manager requests role-based access through Security.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

Do not include a future starter in active headcount before their effective date.
