# Workforce reporting (HR-07)

Owner: Human Resources and Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Active headcount counts each active individual once at the snapshot date. FTE is the sum of contracted work fractions, not headcount. Contractors and future hires are reported separately. Site and departmental totals must reconcile with the employee directory.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A part-time employee at 0.8 FTE contributes one person and 0.8 FTE.
