# Workforce capability initiative — SIN (PRJ-0161)

Initiative started 2025-01-02; accountable department [HR](../departments/HR.md), delivery site [SIN](../sites/SIN.md). Review snapshot 2025-04-02. Project status: delivery at risk. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to increase availability of qualified staff. The baseline measure was 55% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 70%. Measurement definition: percentage of valid role certifications with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Training attendance is not independent safety certification. An expired certification cannot be restored by scheduling the employee on a shift.
