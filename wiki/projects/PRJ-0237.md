# Contract and trade compliance initiative — PHX (PRJ-0237)

Initiative started 2026-05-18; accountable department [LEG](../departments/LEG.md), delivery site [PHX](../sites/PHX.md). Review snapshot 2026-08-18. Project status: delivery at risk. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to reduce incomplete review of nonstandard contracts and exports. The baseline measure was 56% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 77%. Measurement definition: percentage of documented scoped decisions with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Authorization is specific to the approved terms, destination and product scope. Regional commercial pressure does not replace Legal review.
