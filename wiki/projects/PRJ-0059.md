# Commercial renewal initiative — BRN (PRJ-0059)

Initiative started 2023-03-20; accountable department [SAL](../departments/SAL.md), delivery site [BRN](../sites/BRN.md). Review snapshot 2023-06-20. Project status: delivery in progress. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to improve contract renewal evidence and quotation validity. The baseline measure was 53% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 76%. Measurement definition: percentage of reviewed renewal opportunities with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Contract extensions and pricing approvals must be recorded before commitments. Do not treat a forecast opportunity as booked revenue.
