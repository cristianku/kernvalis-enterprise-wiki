# Quality traceability initiative — SIN (PRJ-0110)

Initiative started 2024-02-11; accountable department [QA](../departments/QA.md), delivery site [SIN](../sites/SIN.md). Review snapshot 2024-05-11. Project status: delivery in progress. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to improve identification of affected production and installed units. The baseline measure was 54% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 78%. Measurement definition: percentage of traceable released units with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Retain supplier lot, product identity, work order and shipment references through rework. Contain suspect lots without making unsupported claims about unrelated serials.
