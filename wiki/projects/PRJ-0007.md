## Business case

The sponsor proposed to improve physical and recorded stock agreement. The baseline measure was 51% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 72%. Measurement definition: percentage of reconciled inventory discrepancies with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. In-transit stock is not available at destination, and held stock remains restricted. Do not infer quality clearance from a balance adjustment.
