# Design change governance initiative — PHX (PRJ-0233)

Initiative started 2026-01-14; accountable department [ENG](../departments/ENG.md), delivery site [PHX](../sites/PHX.md). Review snapshot 2026-04-14. Project status: delivery at risk. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to reduce uncontrolled component changes. The baseline measure was 52% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 69%. Measurement definition: percentage of verified change packages with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Product safety impact, revision approval and serial effectivity determine implementation. Purchasing pressure cannot justify early deployment of unapproved revisions.
