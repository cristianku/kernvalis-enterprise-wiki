# Identity governance initiative — PHX (PRJ-0010)

Initiative started 2022-06-11; accountable department [SEC](../departments/SEC.md), delivery site [PHX](../sites/PHX.md). Review snapshot 2022-09-11. Project status: delivery in progress. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to reduce stale privileged access and uncontrolled delegation. The baseline measure was 54% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 78%. Measurement definition: percentage of reviewed privilege assignments with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Privileged access requires separate approval, identity, MFA and expiry where applicable. Do not share credentials to provide absence coverage.
