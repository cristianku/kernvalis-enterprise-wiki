# Capacity expansion initiative — TOR (PRJ-0226)

Initiative started 2026-06-07; accountable department [MFG](../departments/MFG.md), delivery site [TOR](../sites/TOR.md). Review snapshot 2026-09-07. Project status: delivery in progress. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to reduce bottlenecks in assembly and production. The baseline measure was 45% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 65%. Measurement definition: percentage of qualified productive hours with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Asset readiness, operator certification and released material constrain productive capacity. Reject the temptation to book capacity from an asset that has been removed from operation.
