# Credit control improvement initiative — AUS (PRJ-0228)

Initiative started 2026-08-09; accountable department [FIN](../departments/FIN.md), delivery site [AUS](../sites/AUS.md). Review snapshot 2026-11-09. Project status: delivery in progress. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to reduce commercial exposure and unapproved order releases. The baseline measure was 47% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 69%. Measurement definition: percentage of reconciled exposure records with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Unbilled shipments and open orders belong in exposure under the current policy. Protect the separation of customer relationship management and independent Finance approval.
