# Supplier resilience initiative — OSA (PRJ-0212)

Initiative started 2026-08-13; accountable department [PUR](../departments/PUR.md), delivery site [OSA](../sites/OSA.md). Review snapshot 2026-11-13. Project status: delivery in progress. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to reduce exposure to constrained critical component sourcing. The baseline measure was 56% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 72%. Measurement definition: percentage of qualified alternative suppliers with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. An alternate source needs qualification evidence, and substitutions need Engineering and Quality approval. A backup vendor in a contingency plan is not automatically authorized to supply production.
