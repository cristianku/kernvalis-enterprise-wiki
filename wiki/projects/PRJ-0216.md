# Software release controls initiative — OSA (PRJ-0216)

Initiative started 2026-04-17; accountable department [SW](../departments/SW.md), delivery site [OSA](../sites/OSA.md). Review snapshot 2026-07-17. Project status: delivery in progress. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to reduce deployment of incompatible firmware. The baseline measure was 60% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 80%. Measurement definition: percentage of verified compatibility records with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Released version, hardware revision, regression evidence and rollback remain independent. A newer firmware version is not necessarily eligible for every hardware revision.
