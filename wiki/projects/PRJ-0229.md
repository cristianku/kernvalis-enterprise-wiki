## Business case

The sponsor proposed to improve response and reduce incomplete equipment histories. The baseline measure was 48% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 71%. Measurement definition: percentage of serial-linked support records with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy.
