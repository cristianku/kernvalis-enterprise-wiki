# Continuity and recovery initiative — MUC (PRJ-0219)

Initiative started 2026-03-20; accountable department [IT](../departments/IT.md), delivery site [MUC](../sites/MUC.md). Review snapshot 2026-06-20. Project status: delivery in progress. This is a fictional historical initiative record and does not establish general policy for other projects.

## Business case

The sponsor proposed to restore business-critical services with verifiable reconciliation. The baseline measure was 63% of applicable items meeting the defined operational standard; the desired result was 86%. Measurement definition: percentage of verified recovery exercises with complete evidence at the review checkpoint. The project does not change the underlying approval rules.

## Scope and constraints

Included: site process mapping, evidence reconciliation, training and verification of the revised process. Excluded: unrelated changes to other regions and blanket exceptions to corporate policy. Business recovery is distinct from technical recovery and must respect the applicable RPO and RTO. A green service health dashboard does not prove transaction completeness.
