Reviewed incident: [INC-0021](../it-incidents/INC-0021.md). System: [ContractVault](../systems/ContractVault.md). Review recorded 2026-10-11; recovery and review are separate milestones.

The action remains open; incident recovery does not establish remediation completion.

Business recovery objectives depend on the system tier in the service inventory and the [backup policy](../it/backup-recovery.md).
