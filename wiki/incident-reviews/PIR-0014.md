# Post-incident review PIR-0014

Reviewed incident: [INC-0014](../it-incidents/INC-0014.md). System: [ReleaseForge](../systems/ReleaseForge.md). Review recorded 2026-10-07; recovery and review are separate milestones.

Confirmed technical cause: exhausted connection pool. The system owner identified inadequate automated detection of the condition. Corrective action: introduce an alert with a documented escalation owner, test it in a controlled environment and track deployment under the production change process. The action remains open; incident recovery does not establish remediation completion.

Business recovery objectives depend on the system tier in the service inventory and the [backup policy](../it/backup-recovery.md).
