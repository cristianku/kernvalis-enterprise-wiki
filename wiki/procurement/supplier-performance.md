# Supplier performance measurement (PUR-03)

Owner: Procurement and Quality. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

On-time delivery compares actual receipt with the confirmed required date; quality rejection rate uses rejected quantity divided by received quantity. Missing receipts are not counted as successful deliveries. Supplier scores do not override qualification status.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A high delivery score cannot restore an expired conditional qualification.
