# Receipt and invoice matching (PUR-04)

Owner: Procurement and Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Match order number, accepted quantity, agreed price and supplier invoice. A receipt of held material is not acceptance for payment unless a controlled contractual exception is documented. Price variance above 2% requires Procurement and Finance review.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A delivered but rejected critical component lot is not an accepted receipt.
