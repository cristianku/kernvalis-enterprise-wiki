# Purchase approval (PUR-02)

Owner: Procurement and Finance. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Purchases below USD 10,000 require cost-center owner approval. From USD 10,000 through USD 100,000, Procurement manager approval is also required. Above USD 100,000, Finance director approval is required as well. Aggregate split requisitions for one commitment.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
