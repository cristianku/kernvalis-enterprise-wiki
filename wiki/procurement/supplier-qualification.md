# Supplier qualification (PUR-01)

Owner: Procurement and Quality. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Critical suppliers need a process audit in addition to commercial and quality approval. Conditional qualification expires after 90 calendar days. Expired qualifications block new purchase orders; existing orders require documented disposition.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
