# Strategic sourcing resilience (PUR-05)

Owner: Procurement. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Critical single-source components require an annual resilience review, documented contingency and inventory risk assessment. Alternate sourcing requires independent qualification and Engineering approval where design changes apply.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

An unqualified alternative supplier may appear in a contingency plan but may not receive a new production order.
