# Quotation validity (SAL-02)

Owner: Sales. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Standard quotations expire 30 calendar days after issuance unless an approved contract specifies another period. Price, currency, delivery terms and discounts are recorded at acceptance. An expired quotation must be revalidated; a customer purchase order is not itself acceptance of new terms.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

An order placed after quotation expiry requires renewed commercial validation.
