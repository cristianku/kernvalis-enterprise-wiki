# Commercial renewal (SAL-04)

Owner: Sales. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Start review 90 days before contract expiry; verify pricing, service commitments, open disputes and Legal-required provisions. Automatic renewal may be relied on only where the executed contract explicitly provides for it.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A salesperson's forecast of renewal is not an executed extension.
