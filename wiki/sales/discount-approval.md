# Discount approval (SAL-01)

Owner: Sales and Finance. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Up to 10% discount: Sales manager. Above 10% through 20%: regional Sales director. Above 20%: also Finance. Include related rebates. Discount approval does not release credit hold.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
