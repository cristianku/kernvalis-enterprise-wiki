# Order amendment control (SAL-05)

Owner: Sales Operations. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Retain original order and each revision. Increases in exposure require new credit evaluation; requested-date changes require new availability review. Destination changes require applicable compliance revalidation. Cancellation releases reservations but preserves audit records.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

Changing a destination from domestic to international requires the corresponding export review.
