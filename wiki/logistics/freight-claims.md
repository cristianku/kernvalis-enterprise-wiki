# Freight claims (LOG-03)

Owner: Logistics. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Visible damage must be reported within 48 elapsed hours; concealed damage within seven calendar days. Preserve carrier references and packaging evidence. Warranty and carrier liability are separate.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
