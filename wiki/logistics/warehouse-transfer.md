# Interwarehouse transfers (LOG-05)

Owner: Logistics. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Transfers have origin dispatch and destination receipt records. In-transit inventory is not physically available at the destination. A held lot retains its hold and traceability throughout the transfer.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A transfer dispatched but not received cannot be used as destination available-to-promise stock.
