# Export release (LOG-02)

Owner: Logistics and Legal & Compliance. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

International shipments require classification, destination screening and applicable licensing evidence. A compliance hold prevents shipment independently of credit or stock.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
