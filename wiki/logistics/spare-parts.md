# Service spare-parts allocation (LOG-06)

Owner: Logistics and Customer Service. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Protected service stock may be released under approved regional logistics exception. Safety-critical service demands are prioritized after eligibility checks. Service priority is not authority to override product holds.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A P1 ticket does not itself authorize shipment of a quarantined part.
