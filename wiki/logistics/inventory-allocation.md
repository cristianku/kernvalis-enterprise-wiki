# Inventory allocation (LOG-AL-01)

Owner: Global Logistics. Effective 2025-07-01.

Available-to-promise quantity equals physically available stock minus existing reservations, quality holds and protected safety stock. Expected inbound deliveries are not considered physical stock.

Allocation normally follows requested ship date, then order acceptance timestamp. Safety stock can be released only by the regional Logistics director with Quality concurrence for regulated product lines. Allocation expires after 48 hours on orders that remain on credit hold; the inventory is returned to the available pool.

Warehouse personnel must revalidate the allocation and credit release before dispatch. See [order management](../processes/order-management.md).
