# Inventory cycle counting (LOG-04)

Owner: Logistics and Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Record physical quantity, book quantity, variance and recount evidence. Adjustments above USD 5,000 require Finance approval. Quality holds and safety stock must not disappear through a stock count adjustment.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A recount changes recorded quantity only after approved adjustment; it cannot release a quality restriction.
