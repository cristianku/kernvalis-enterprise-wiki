# Identity offboarding (SEC-02)

Owner: Security and Human Resources. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Disable interactive access by effective termination time, even if paperwork is incomplete for involuntary termination. Transfer service-account ownership instead of blindly disabling accounts.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
