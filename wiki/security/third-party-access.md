# External access (SEC-05)

Owner: Security and Procurement. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

External access requires sponsor, purpose, minimum privilege and expiry. Review access at contract termination and revoke when no longer required. No shared privileged identity for vendors.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

Supplier qualification does not automatically grant access to engineering documents.
