# Information classification (SEC-04)

Owner: Security. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Classify information as public, internal, confidential or restricted. Personal information, authentication secrets and unannounced design details require applicable restricted handling. Classification governs access and distribution, not historical retention.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A public product data sheet may be shared while its underlying confidential validation records remain controlled.
