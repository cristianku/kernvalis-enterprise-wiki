# Steering committee minutes — PRJ-0033

Meeting 2022-01-29; sponsor FIN; site DET. Attendees by role: sponsor, Finance representative, site delivery lead, independent control representative. Minutes approved as a record of discussion only.

**Sponsor:** the proposed improvement should reduce commercial exposure and unapproved order releases; baseline evidence covers the sampled operational records, not all company transactions.

**Independent reviewer:** Unbilled shipments and open orders belong in exposure under the current policy. The revised process must preserve separate approval authority. Requested evidence of a representative negative case, not only a successful demonstration.

**Finance:** the business case includes equipment and implementation. The sponsor asked whether the two costs could be approved separately; Finance rejected treating them as unrelated commitments. The final investment disposition is recorded in [decision](../investment-decisions/PRJ-0033.md).

**Actions:** delivery lead prepares validation plan; sponsor documents scope; Finance confirms approval route; control owner reviews unresolved exceptions. No independent approval was granted by these minutes. See [risk register](../project-risks/PRJ-0033.md).
