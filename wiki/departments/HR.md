# Human Resources — departmental charter

Charter ID: DEPT-HR. Effective 2026-01-01. Corporate home: Austin, Texas. Scope: all subsidiaries and operational sites where the function is represented.

## Mandate

Recruiting, employment lifecycle, learning, employee relations and workforce planning. Maintain effective-dated workforce records and coordinate role certification and offboarding. The department is responsible for the correctness of its own records and must not silently alter decisions owned by a different function.

## Interfaces and decision rights

Primary coordination partners: Security; Finance; Manufacturing. Cross-functional disagreements are recorded in the accountable process owner's decision record. Site execution authority does not supersede corporate policy. When approval authority is absent, staff must escalate rather than infer authorization from a job title.

## Records and performance

Headcount, qualification and employment events. Operational evidence must identify the transaction, decision owner, date and supporting source. The corporate calendar requires monthly reporting and a quarterly review of unresolved risks. Sensitive employee and commercial information is available only to authorized roles in the fictional enterprise.
