# Executive — departmental charter

Charter ID: DEPT-EXE. Effective 2026-01-01. Corporate home: Austin, Texas. Scope: all subsidiaries and operational sites where the function is represented.

## Mandate

Corporate strategy, board secretariat, capital allocation and enterprise risk. Approve strategic priorities, appoint accountable executives, chair the investment council. The department is responsible for the correctness of its own records and must not silently alter decisions owned by a different function.

## Interfaces and decision rights

Primary coordination partners: Finance; Legal & Compliance. Cross-functional disagreements are recorded in the accountable process owner's decision record. Site execution authority does not supersede corporate policy. When approval authority is absent, staff must escalate rather than infer authorization from a job title.

## Records and performance

Strategic decisions and approved investment mandates. Operational evidence must identify the transaction, decision owner, date and supporting source. The corporate calendar requires monthly reporting and a quarterly review of unresolved risks. Sensitive employee and commercial information is available only to authorized roles in the fictional enterprise.

[AUS](../sites/AUS.md). Local teams report to their respective corporate function for policy and to the site for operational coordination. Refer to [organization](../company/organization.md) and the [governance charter](../governance/decision-rights.md).
