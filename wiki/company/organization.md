The 15 departments are Executive, Finance, Sales, Marketing, Customer Service, Engineering, Software, Manufacturing, Quality, Procurement, Logistics, IT, Security, Human Resources and Legal & Compliance. Finance owns commercial credit policy; Logistics owns stock allocation; Sales owns order intake. Site managers cannot override corporate credit controls.
