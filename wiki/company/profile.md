# Company profile

Kernvalis Industries, Inc. designs and supplies industrial robotics, motion controllers, sensors and factory integration services. Founded in 2005 in Austin, Texas, the company grew from a motion-control engineering business into a multinational manufacturer. The November 2026 planning baseline is 2,500 employees, 12 sites and annual revenue of USD 850 million (fictional planning figures).

The company sells through direct enterprise contracts and authorized distributors. Three product families are VectorMotion servo systems, AxisForge industrial robots and SignalSpan sensors. Service contracts cover commissioning, maintenance and replacement parts. The corporate fiscal year ends December 31.
