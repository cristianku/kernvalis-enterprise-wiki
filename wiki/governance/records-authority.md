# Evidence and document authority (GOV-05)

Owner: Legal & Compliance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

A current signed policy governs general requirements. Effective-dated approved exceptions apply only within their scope and validity. A transaction record establishes recorded facts; a draft, training note or meeting discussion cannot silently supersede a policy. For historical questions use the rule applicable on the event date. If evidence is irreconcilable, disclose the conflict rather than choose arbitrarily.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A 2024 training deck may correctly describe a former threshold yet be invalid for a 2026 decision.
