# Enterprise risk acceptance (GOV-04)

Owner: Executive. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Residual risk must be rated by impact and likelihood, with a named owner, mitigation and review date. High residual risks require executive acceptance and an expiry no later than 180 days. Risk acceptance cannot override a product safety prohibition, legal requirement or mandatory segregation of duties.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

An approved risk exception is not a substitute for an export license.
