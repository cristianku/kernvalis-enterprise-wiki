# Corporate decision rights (GOV-01)

Owner: Executive and Legal & Compliance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

A decision requires an accountable business owner, approval within delegated limits, evidence of review and an effective date. A recommendation is not authorization. Site managers own local execution; Finance owns credit, Quality owns product release, Legal & Compliance owns export release, Security owns privileged access. An executive escalation can require review but does not itself satisfy a legally or procedurally required independent approval.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

An order with commercial approval but an open Quality hold cannot be shipped. Record each independent release separately.
