# Board and executive cadence (GOV-02)

Owner: Executive. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

The board reviews strategy and material investment quarterly. Executive operating reviews occur monthly and site reviews weekly. Board resolutions specify a resolution number, approving body, date and action owner. A discussion in meeting minutes is not a board resolution unless explicitly identified as approved.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

Deferred agenda items retain their previous decision state until formally resolved.
