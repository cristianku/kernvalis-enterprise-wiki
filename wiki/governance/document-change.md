# Controlled document changes (GOV-07)

Owner: Legal & Compliance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

A controlled document revision identifies author, approval, effective date and superseded version. Drafts are clearly marked. Preserve previous effective versions for historical analysis and legal holds. A hyperlink alone does not establish which revision applied to a past decision.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

Use the policy effective on the event date for retrospective compliance assessment.
