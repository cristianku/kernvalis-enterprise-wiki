# Capital investment governance (GOV-03)

Owner: Executive and Finance. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Capital requests below USD 250,000 require the business sponsor and Finance director. From USD 250,000 through USD 2 million, the investment council must also approve. Above USD 2 million, board authorization is required in addition. Total lifecycle investment includes equipment, implementation and capitalizable deployment expenditure; related phases cannot be split to bypass approval. Benefits forecasts do not authorize commitments.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A USD 1.8 million equipment request plus USD 400,000 implementation requires board approval.
