# Business continuity planning (GOV-06)

Owner: Executive and IT. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Each site must maintain a continuity plan with critical operations, dependency mapping, manual workarounds, escalation contacts and reconciliation procedures. Exercise annually. A workaround cannot bypass Quality, credit or trade compliance controls. Recovery requires reconciliation of transactions created during the interruption.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

During an ERP outage, prepare orders manually but do not dispatch until required release evidence is verified.
