# Product lifecycle management (ENG-03)

Owner: Engineering and Sales. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Product status transitions through development, released, restricted, end-of-sale and end-of-support. End-of-sale stops new standard bookings after its effective date; existing service obligations continue according to contracts. Product retirement requires approved communication and spare-parts planning.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

End-of-sale is not the date on which warranty coverage ends.
