# Component substitution (ENG-04)

Owner: Engineering and Quality. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

An alternate component requires documented form, fit, function and safety evaluation, plus approved BOM effectivity. Procurement may propose substitutions but cannot authorize production use. Temporary deviations specify affected work orders, quantity and expiration.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

Supplier availability does not justify replacing a safety-critical connector without approval.
