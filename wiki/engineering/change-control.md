# Engineering change control (ENG-01)

Owner: Engineering. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Changes require impact assessment, verification and approved revision. Safety changes require Quality concurrence. Serial-number effectivity determines applicability.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
