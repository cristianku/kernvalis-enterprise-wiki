# Design review and verification (ENG-02)

Owner: Engineering. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Design reviews require documented requirements, verification strategy, open risks and approval before release. A prototype may be tested under a controlled plan but is not a production-approved revision. Safety-critical changes require independent Quality concurrence.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

Successful prototype operation does not release a new production BOM.
