# Incoming inspection (QA-01)

Owner: Quality. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Critical component lots remain held until inspection is accepted. A rejected inspection blocks the affected lot, not all lots of the same SKU. Receiving staff cannot release rejected lots.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
