# Measurement equipment calibration (QA-03)

Owner: Quality. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Measurement devices used for product acceptance must be in calibration at measurement time. Expired or out-of-tolerance calibration triggers impact assessment of measurements since the last valid check. Recalibration alone does not establish acceptability of earlier products.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

If a gauge is overdue, preserve readings and reassess affected lots before release.
