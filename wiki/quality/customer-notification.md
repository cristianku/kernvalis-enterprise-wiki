# Customer quality notification (QA-06)

Owner: Quality and Customer Service. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

When a verified safety defect affects shipped units, Quality defines the affected population and Customer Service coordinates notifications. Legal reviews external safety communications. Unaffected serials must not be included without evidence.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A single affected lot is not proof that all product revisions are defective.
