# Corrective and preventive action (QA-04)

Owner: Quality. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Safety-related or recurrent confirmed defects require containment, root-cause analysis, corrective action, verification of effectiveness and closure approval. Three distinct serials with the same confirmed manufacturing defect in 30 days trigger investigation under SVC-03. A temporary workaround is not verified corrective action.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

Closing a service ticket does not close the associated corrective action.
