# Product and lot traceability (QA-05)

Owner: Quality. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Maintain linkage from supplier lot to production lot, serialized finished unit and shipment where serial tracking applies. Rework retains the original identity and records disposition. If material identity is lost, quarantine affected units until traceability is re-established or disposition approved.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A component recall requires identifying exposed finished units, not every unit of the product family.
