# PHX Software team

Team ID: TEAM-PHX-SW. Site: [PHX](../sites/PHX.md). Function: [SW](../departments/SW.md). Directory allocation: 28 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Embedded software, firmware, diagnostic applications and validation tooling. Maintain supported firmware baselines and release compatibility records. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Engineering; Security; Quality. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/PHX-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
