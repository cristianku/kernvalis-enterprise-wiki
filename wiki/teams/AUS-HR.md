# AUS Human Resources team

Team ID: TEAM-AUS-HR. Site: [AUS](../sites/AUS.md). Function: [HR](../departments/HR.md). Directory allocation: 32 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Recruiting, employment lifecycle, learning, employee relations and workforce planning. Maintain effective-dated workforce records and coordinate role certification and offboarding. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Security; Finance; Manufacturing. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/AUS-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
