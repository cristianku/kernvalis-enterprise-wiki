# SIN Customer Service team

Team ID: TEAM-SIN-SVC. Site: [SIN](../sites/SIN.md). Function: [SVC](../departments/SVC.md). Directory allocation: 12 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Field assistance, customer support, warranty decisions, returns and service contracts. Triage support issues, maintain equipment history and coordinate warranty and safety returns. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Quality; Engineering; Logistics. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/SIN-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
