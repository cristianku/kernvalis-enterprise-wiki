# SIN Quality team

Team ID: TEAM-SIN-QA. Site: [SIN](../sites/SIN.md). Function: [QA](../departments/QA.md). Directory allocation: 12 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Supplier quality, production inspection, product traceability, corrective action and customer quality. Control product release and nonconformance disposition; own independent quality stop authority. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Procurement; Manufacturing; Customer Service. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/SIN-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
