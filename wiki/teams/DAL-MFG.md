# DAL Manufacturing team

Team ID: TEAM-DAL-MFG. Site: [DAL](../sites/DAL.md). Function: [MFG](../departments/MFG.md). Directory allocation: 70 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Production scheduling, assembly, maintenance and operator certification coordination. Execute approved work orders; stop work on unsafe assets or unreleased materials. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Quality; Logistics; Engineering. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/DAL-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
