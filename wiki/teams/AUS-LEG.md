# AUS Legal & Compliance team

Team ID: TEAM-AUS-LEG. Site: [AUS](../sites/AUS.md). Function: [LEG](../departments/LEG.md). Directory allocation: 32 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Contract review, trade compliance, retention, ethics and investigations. Review nonstandard commitments, enforce legal holds and independent export clearance. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Executive; Sales; Security. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/AUS-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
