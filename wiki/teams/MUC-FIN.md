# MUC Finance team

Team ID: TEAM-MUC-FIN. Site: [MUC](../sites/MUC.md). Function: [FIN](../departments/FIN.md). Directory allocation: 16 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Controllership, treasury, credit, financial planning, payroll accounting and internal controls. Own the financial ledger, credit decisions, financial close and budgets; separate authorization from posting. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Sales; Procurement; Human Resources. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/MUC-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
