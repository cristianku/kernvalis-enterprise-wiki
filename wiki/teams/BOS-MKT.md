# BOS Marketing team

Team ID: TEAM-BOS-MKT. Site: [BOS](../sites/BOS.md). Function: [MKT](../departments/MKT.md). Directory allocation: 18 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Product positioning, campaigns, competitive intelligence and events. Validate product claims before publication; manage launch calendars and lead qualification. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Engineering; Sales; Legal & Compliance. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/BOS-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
