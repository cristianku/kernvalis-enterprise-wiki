# AUS IT team

Team ID: TEAM-AUS-IT. Site: [AUS](../sites/AUS.md). Function: [IT](../departments/IT.md). Directory allocation: 32 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Enterprise applications, infrastructure, service operations and disaster recovery. Maintain operational availability and evidence of controlled production changes. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Security; Software; Finance. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/AUS-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
