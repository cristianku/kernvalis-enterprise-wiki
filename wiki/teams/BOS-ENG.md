# BOS Engineering team

Team ID: TEAM-BOS-ENG. Site: [BOS](../sites/BOS.md). Function: [ENG](../departments/ENG.md). Directory allocation: 19 positions including the team lead, excluding the site manager.

## Work and responsibilities

Mechanical design, electronics, product safety, BOM and engineering changes. Maintain design baselines, validate engineering changes and manage serial effectivity. Team members maintain handover notes for open work, assign a responsible owner to every exception and record business decisions in the appropriate source system. A team lead coordinates work and approves within delegated limits only.

## Coordination

The local team works with Software; Quality; Manufacturing. Site operating issues are raised to the site manager; functional policy issues are raised through the corporate department. Local delegation does not grant authority to release independent holds.

See [site charter](../site-operations/BOS-charter.md), [corporate decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [corporate organization](../company/organization.md).
