# Incident INC-0032: BackupVault interruption

Affected service: [BackupVault](../systems/BackupVault.md). Opened: 2026-10-16T08:00:00Z. Severity: 1. Critical business workflow unavailable; no workaround. Incident commander assigned 12 minutes after opening.

The incident is distinct from the corrective action review: [PIR-0032](../incident-reviews/PIR-0032.md). See [IT-01](../it/incident-management.md).
