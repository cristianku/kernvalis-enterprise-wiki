# Incident INC-0012: DesignVault interruption

Affected service: [DesignVault](../systems/DesignVault.md). Opened: 2026-10-06T08:00:00Z. Severity: 1. Critical business workflow unavailable; no workaround. Incident commander assigned 12 minutes after opening.

Observed condition: invalid configuration rollout. Service restored after 195 elapsed minutes. The recovery team measured 25 minutes of unrecoverable updates relative to the pre-incident state. The business owner was notified to reconcile affected transactions.

The incident is distinct from the corrective action review: [PIR-0012](../incident-reviews/PIR-0012.md). See [IT-01](../it/incident-management.md).
