# Incident INC-0003: StockFlow interruption

Affected service: [StockFlow](../systems/StockFlow.md). Opened: 2026-10-02T08:00:00Z. Severity: 1. Critical business workflow unavailable; no workaround. Incident commander assigned 22 minutes after opening.

Observed condition: failed storage controller. Service restored after 130 elapsed minutes. The recovery team measured 12 minutes of unrecoverable updates relative to the pre-incident state. The business owner was notified to reconcile affected transactions.

The incident is distinct from the corrective action review: [PIR-0003](../incident-reviews/PIR-0003.md). See [IT-01](../it/incident-management.md).
