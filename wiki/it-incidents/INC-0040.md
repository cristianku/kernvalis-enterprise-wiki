# Incident INC-0040: FreightTrack interruption

Affected service: [FreightTrack](../systems/FreightTrack.md). Opened: 2026-10-20T08:00:00Z. Severity: 2. Major degradation; temporary manual workaround used. Incident commander assigned 16 minutes after opening.

Observed condition: incorrect DNS record. Service restored after 110 elapsed minutes. The recovery team measured 25 minutes of unrecoverable updates relative to the pre-incident state. The business owner was notified to reconcile affected transactions.

The incident is distinct from the corrective action review: [PIR-0040](../incident-reviews/PIR-0040.md). See [IT-01](../it/incident-management.md).
