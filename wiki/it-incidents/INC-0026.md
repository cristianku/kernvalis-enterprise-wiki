# Incident INC-0026: IdentityHub interruption

Affected service: [IdentityHub](../systems/IdentityHub.md). Opened: 2026-10-13T08:00:00Z. Severity: 1. Critical business workflow unavailable; no workaround. Incident commander assigned 8 minutes after opening.

Observed condition: stalled message queue. Service restored after 85 elapsed minutes. The recovery team measured 25 minutes of unrecoverable updates relative to the pre-incident state. The business owner was notified to reconcile affected transactions.

The incident is distinct from the corrective action review: [PIR-0026](../incident-reviews/PIR-0026.md). See [IT-01](../it/incident-management.md).
