# Incident INC-0002: OrderDesk interruption

Affected service: [OrderDesk](../systems/OrderDesk.md). Opened: 2026-10-01T08:00:00Z. Severity: 1. Critical business workflow unavailable; no workaround. Incident commander assigned 12 minutes after opening.

Observed condition: stalled message queue. Service restored after 85 elapsed minutes. The recovery team measured 5 minutes of unrecoverable updates relative to the pre-incident state. The business owner was notified to reconcile affected transactions.

The incident is distinct from the corrective action review: [PIR-0002](../incident-reviews/PIR-0002.md). See [IT-01](../it/incident-management.md).
