# Incident INC-0010: AssetTrack interruption

Affected service: [AssetTrack](../systems/AssetTrack.md). Opened: 2026-10-05T08:00:00Z. Severity: 2. Major degradation; temporary manual workaround used. Incident commander assigned 16 minutes after opening.

Observed condition: stalled message queue. Service restored after 85 elapsed minutes. The recovery team measured 12 minutes of unrecoverable updates relative to the pre-incident state. The business owner was notified to reconcile affected transactions.

The incident is distinct from the corrective action review: [PIR-0010](../incident-reviews/PIR-0010.md). See [IT-01](../it/incident-management.md).
