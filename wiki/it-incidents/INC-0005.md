# Incident INC-0005: DispatchLink interruption

Affected service: [DispatchLink](../systems/DispatchLink.md). Opened: 2026-10-03T08:00:00Z. Severity: 1. Critical business workflow unavailable; no workaround. Incident commander assigned 16 minutes after opening.

Observed condition: certificate chain mismatch. Service restored after 260 elapsed minutes. The recovery team measured 25 minutes of unrecoverable updates relative to the pre-incident state. The business owner was notified to reconcile affected transactions.

The incident is distinct from the corrective action review: [PIR-0005](../incident-reviews/PIR-0005.md). See [IT-01](../it/incident-management.md).
