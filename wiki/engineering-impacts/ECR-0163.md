# Impact assessment — ECR-0163

Assessment for [engineering proposal](../engineering-proposals/ECR-0163.md). Design objective: Reduce intermittent encoder alarms. Signal filtering may affect diagnostic timing and response. Independent review covers procurement availability, affected drawings, material acceptance, service diagnostics, spare-parts compatibility and production instructions.

Quality concurrence is mandatory for the proposed safety-related change. Existing installed units and earlier serials are not automatically within scope. The proposal identifies a reserved pilot serial range beginning KVI-PILOT-116200; no customer serial is identified as affected.

Verification required: signal repeatability, diagnostic response and regression. No broad production substitution was requested. See [ENG-01](../engineering/change-control.md), [ENG-04](../engineering/component-substitution.md) and [design review](../engineering/design-review.md).
