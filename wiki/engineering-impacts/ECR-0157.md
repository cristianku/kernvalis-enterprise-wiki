# Impact assessment — ECR-0157

Assessment for [engineering proposal](../engineering-proposals/ECR-0157.md). Design objective: Reduce moisture sensitivity. A coating change may affect rework and inspection acceptance. Independent review covers procurement availability, affected drawings, material acceptance, service diagnostics, spare-parts compatibility and production instructions.

Quality concurrence is mandatory for the proposed safety-related change. Existing installed units and earlier serials are not automatically within scope. The proposal identifies a reserved pilot serial range beginning KVI-PILOT-115600; no customer serial is identified as affected.

Verification required: environmental conditioning, insulation and rework compatibility. No broad production substitution was requested. See [ENG-01](../engineering/change-control.md), [ENG-04](../engineering/component-substitution.md) and [design review](../engineering/design-review.md).
