# Impact assessment — ECR-0146

Assessment for [engineering proposal](../engineering-proposals/ECR-0146.md). Design objective: Improve thermal transfer. The alternate thickness changes assembly stack-up; evaluate fit before effectivity. Independent review covers procurement availability, affected drawings, material acceptance, service diagnostics, spare-parts compatibility and production instructions.

The change was classified as standard; engineering verification and documented approval remain mandatory. Existing installed units and earlier serials are not automatically within scope. The proposal identifies a reserved pilot serial range beginning KVI-PILOT-114500; no customer serial is identified as affected.

Verification required: thermal cycling and mechanical fit. No broad production substitution was requested. See [ENG-01](../engineering/change-control.md), [ENG-04](../engineering/component-substitution.md) and [design review](../engineering/design-review.md).
