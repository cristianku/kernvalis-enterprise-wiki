# Contract review (LEG-02)

Owner: Legal & Compliance. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Nonstandard liability, exclusive distribution and intellectual property transfers require Legal review before signature. Discounts follow independent approval limits.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
