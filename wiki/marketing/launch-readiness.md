# Product launch readiness (MKT-02)

Owner: Marketing. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Launch readiness requires approved product status, verified public claims, sales training, service preparation and availability communication. Launch communications must not promise unapproved functionality or dates.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A demonstration prototype may be described as such but cannot be advertised as a released product.
