# Product claims (MKT-01)

Owner: Marketing and Engineering. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Published performance claims require approved validation. Comparative safety claims require Legal review. Withdrawn claims must be removed from future materials.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
