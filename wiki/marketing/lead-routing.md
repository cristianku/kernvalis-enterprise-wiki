# Lead qualification and routing (MKT-03)

Owner: Marketing and Sales. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Route qualified leads using account ownership, territory and product interest. Consent and source records remain attached. Duplicate leads merge only after identity verification; do not count duplicates as new opportunities.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A campaign lead is not a booked order or revenue.
