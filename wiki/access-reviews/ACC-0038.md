# Access audit checkpoint ACC-0038

Snapshot: 2026-11-26T12:00:00Z. Sources: [request](../access-requests/ACC-0038.md), [decision](../access-decisions/ACC-0038.md), [entitlement events](../access-events/ACC-0038.md).

The scoped entitlement remains recorded as active and must be included in the applicable periodic review.

No independent evidence of actual use, data loss or unauthorized transactions is included. Do not infer an incident or successful abuse solely from an access-control exception. Retain audit evidence under [SEC-06](../security/log-retention.md); see [SEC-01](../security/access-control.md).
