# Engineering change request ECR-0084 — Housing fastener material

Opened 2026-09-04. Product [SS-290](../products/SS-290.md); current master revision B, proposed revision C. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Address corrosion in exposed installations. Impact: The alternative material requires mechanical and environmental validation.

Safety-impact classification: standard change. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0084.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0084.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0084.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0084.md).
