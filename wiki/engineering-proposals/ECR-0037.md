# Engineering change request ECR-0037 — Board coating

Opened 2026-04-17. Product [VM-260](../products/VM-260.md); current master revision B, proposed revision C. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Reduce moisture sensitivity. Impact: A coating change may affect rework and inspection acceptance.

Safety-impact classification: safety-related. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0037.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0037.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0037.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0037.md).
