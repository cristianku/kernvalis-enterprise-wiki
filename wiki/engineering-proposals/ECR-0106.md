# Engineering change request ECR-0106 — Thermal pad thickness

Opened 2026-03-06. Product [VM-250](../products/VM-250.md); current master revision A, proposed revision B. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Improve thermal transfer. Impact: The alternate thickness changes assembly stack-up; evaluate fit before effectivity.

Safety-impact classification: safety-related. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0106.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0106.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0106.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0106.md).
