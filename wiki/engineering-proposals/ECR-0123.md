# Engineering change request ECR-0123 — Encoder signal filter

Opened 2026-06-03. Product [SS-180](../products/SS-180.md); current master revision C, proposed revision D. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Reduce intermittent encoder alarms. Impact: Signal filtering may affect diagnostic timing and response.

Safety-impact classification: standard change. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0123.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0123.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0123.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0123.md).
