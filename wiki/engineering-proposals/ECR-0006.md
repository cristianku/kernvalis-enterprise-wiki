# Engineering change request ECR-0006 — Bearing tolerance

Opened 2026-08-06. Product [SS-110](../products/SS-110.md); current master revision B, proposed revision C. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Reduce excessive vibration. Impact: Tolerance changes affect assembly and rotational accuracy.

Safety-impact classification: standard change. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0006.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0006.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0006.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0006.md).
