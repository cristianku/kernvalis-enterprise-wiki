# Engineering change request ECR-0145 — Connector retention

Opened 2026-07-05. Product [VM-140](../products/VM-140.md); current master revision B, proposed revision C. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Improve retention under vibration. Impact: A connector design change may affect safety-related electrical continuity.

Safety-impact classification: safety-related. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0145.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0145.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0145.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0145.md).
