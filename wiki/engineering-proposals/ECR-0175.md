# Engineering change request ECR-0175 — Optical calibration

Opened 2026-09-15. Product [VM-240](../products/VM-240.md); current master revision C, proposed revision D. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Improve measurement stability. Impact: Calibration changes affect acceptance criteria and diagnostic interpretation.

Safety-impact classification: safety-related. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0175.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0175.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0175.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0175.md).
