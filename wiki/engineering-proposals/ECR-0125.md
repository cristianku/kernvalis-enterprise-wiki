# Engineering change request ECR-0125 — Board coating

Opened 2026-08-05. Product [AF-270](../products/AF-270.md); current master revision C, proposed revision D. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Reduce moisture sensitivity. Impact: A coating change may affect rework and inspection acceptance.

Safety-impact classification: standard change. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0125.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0125.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0125.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0125.md).
