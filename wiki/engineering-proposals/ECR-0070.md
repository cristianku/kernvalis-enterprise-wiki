# Engineering change request ECR-0070 — Bearing tolerance

Opened 2026-09-10. Product [VM-290](../products/VM-290.md); current master revision B, proposed revision C. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Reduce excessive vibration. Impact: Tolerance changes affect assembly and rotational accuracy.

Safety-impact classification: safety-related. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0070.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0070.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0070.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0070.md).
