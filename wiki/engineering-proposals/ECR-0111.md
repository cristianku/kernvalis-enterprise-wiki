# Engineering change request ECR-0111 — Optical calibration

Opened 2026-08-11. Product [SS-260](../products/SS-260.md); current master revision B, proposed revision C. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Improve measurement stability. Impact: Calibration changes affect acceptance criteria and diagnostic interpretation.

Safety-impact classification: standard change. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0111.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0111.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0111.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0111.md).
