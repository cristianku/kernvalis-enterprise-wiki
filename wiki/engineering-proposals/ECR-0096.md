# Engineering change request ECR-0096 — Communication timeout

Opened 2026-07-16. Product [SS-210](../products/SS-210.md); current master revision C, proposed revision D. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Improve recovery after transient network loss. Impact: Timeout changes affect integration behavior and compatibility.

Safety-impact classification: standard change. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.
