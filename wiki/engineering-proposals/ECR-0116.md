# Engineering change request ECR-0116 — Housing fastener material

Opened 2026-06-16. Product [AF-280](../products/AF-280.md); current master revision A, proposed revision B. This change request is a proposal or restricted pilot; it does not replace the current released product master. Objective: Address corrosion in exposed installations. Impact: The alternative material requires mechanical and environmental validation.

Safety-impact classification: standard change. Affected production and service populations must be determined by controlled serial effectivity, not merely product family. The sponsor requested a controlled pilot before considering general production release.

See [impact assessment](../engineering-impacts/ECR-0116.md), [verification report](../engineering-verifications/VER-0116.md), [review decision](../engineering-decisions/ECR-0116.md) and [pilot record](../engineering-pilots/ECR-0116.md).
