# Software support lifecycle (SW-03)

Owner: Software. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

Supported branches receive applicable defect and security fixes until the documented end-of-support date. Emergency fixes to unsupported branches require an approved exception. Withdrawn builds are not supported releases.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A customer contract can extend service obligations but does not change a withdrawn build's eligibility.
