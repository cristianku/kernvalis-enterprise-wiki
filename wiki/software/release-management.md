# Firmware release management (SW-01)

Owner: Software and Quality. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Production releases require signatures, regression tests and Quality approval. Withdrawn releases may not be installed. Emergency patches require a rollback plan. Hardware compatibility is checked separately.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
