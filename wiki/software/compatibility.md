# Firmware compatibility matrix (SW-02)

Owner: Software and Engineering. Approved revision 1.0; effective 2026-01-01 unless a separate historical record states otherwise. Current at 2026-11-26.

## Scope and rule

A firmware release is eligible only for hardware revisions explicitly listed in its approved compatibility record. Version ordering does not imply compatibility. Field upgrade requires applicable release status and backup of configuration.

## Operating evidence

Capture the business identifier, triggering event, decision date, approving function, cited source and any exception scope. Keep rejected, deferred and expired decisions in the historical record. The record of execution must distinguish the requested action from authorization and from the actual outcome.

## Worked example

A newer release withdrawn for a specific revision must not be installed merely because its version number is higher.
