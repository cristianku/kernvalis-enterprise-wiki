# Project risk register — PRJ-0136

Review date: 2024-05-02. Project: [PRJ-0136](../projects/PRJ-0136.md). Risk owner: MFG site team. Scope: capacity expansion.

### R1 — evidence gap

Asset readiness, operator certification and released material constrain productive capacity. Impact: medium. Likelihood: low. Proposed mitigation: compare operational records against the authoritative control before releasing the affected workflow. Residual risk: medium. The owner accepted monitoring with a scheduled review.

### R2 — implementation drift

Reject the temptation to book capacity from an asset that has been removed from operation. Mitigation: preserve scope, revision, owner and execution evidence. Test the operational handover with the receiving team. Open until the delivery report includes verification.

### R3 — benefits overstatement

The 75% target is a forecast. Use the actual measured result in the benefits review, not the target. No record establishes avoided financial losses.
