Review date: 2025-05-02. Project: [PRJ-0176](../projects/PRJ-0176.md). Risk owner: HR site team. Scope: workforce capability.

### R1 — evidence gap

Training attendance is not independent safety certification. Impact: medium. Likelihood: low. Proposed mitigation: compare operational records against the authoritative control before releasing the affected workflow. Residual risk: medium. The owner accepted monitoring with a scheduled review.

### R2 — implementation drift

An expired certification cannot be restored by scheduling the employee on a shift. Mitigation: preserve scope, revision, owner and execution evidence. Test the operational handover with the receiving team. Open until the delivery report includes verification.

The 65% target is a forecast. Use the actual measured result in the benefits review, not the target. No record establishes avoided financial losses.
