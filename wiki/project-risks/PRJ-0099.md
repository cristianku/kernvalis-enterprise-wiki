# Project risk register — PRJ-0099

Review date: 2024-04-04. Project: [PRJ-0099](../projects/PRJ-0099.md). Risk owner: IT site team. Scope: continuity and recovery.

### R1 — evidence gap

Business recovery is distinct from technical recovery and must respect the applicable RPO and RTO. Impact: medium. Likelihood: low. Proposed mitigation: compare operational records against the authoritative control before releasing the affected workflow. Residual risk: medium. The owner accepted monitoring with a scheduled review.

### R2 — implementation drift

A green service health dashboard does not prove transaction completeness. Mitigation: preserve scope, revision, owner and execution evidence. Test the operational handover with the receiving team. Open until the delivery report includes verification.

### R3 — benefits overstatement

The 91% target is a forecast. Use the actual measured result in the benefits review, not the target. No record establishes avoided financial losses.
