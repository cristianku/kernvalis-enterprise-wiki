Proposed mitigation: compare operational records against the authoritative control before releasing the affected workflow. Residual risk: high. Executive risk acceptance has not been recorded in this register. A separate approval would be required where applicable.

Reject the temptation to book capacity from an asset that has been removed from operation. Mitigation: preserve scope, revision, owner and execution evidence. Test the operational handover with the receiving team. Open until the delivery report includes verification.

The 60% target is a forecast. Use the actual measured result in the benefits review, not the target. No record establishes avoided financial losses.
