# Project risk register — PRJ-0032

Review date: 2022-04-28. Project: [PRJ-0032](../projects/PRJ-0032.md). Risk owner: PUR site team. Scope: supplier resilience.

### R1 — evidence gap

An alternate source needs qualification evidence, and substitutions need Engineering and Quality approval. Impact: medium. Likelihood: low. Proposed mitigation: compare operational records against the authoritative control before releasing the affected workflow. Residual risk: medium. The owner accepted monitoring with a scheduled review.

### R2 — implementation drift

A backup vendor in a contingency plan is not automatically authorized to supply production. Mitigation: preserve scope, revision, owner and execution evidence. Test the operational handover with the receiving team. Open until the delivery report includes verification.

### R3 — benefits overstatement

The 67% target is a forecast. Use the actual measured result in the benefits review, not the target. No record establishes avoided financial losses.

See [risk governance](../governance/risk-acceptance.md) and [benefits review](../project-benefits/PRJ-0032.md).
