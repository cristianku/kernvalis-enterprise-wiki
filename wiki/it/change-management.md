# Production changes (IT-02)

Owner: IT and Security. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Production changes require test evidence, an approved window and rollback instructions. Emergency changes require retrospective review within two business days.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
