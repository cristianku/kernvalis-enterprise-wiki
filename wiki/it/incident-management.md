# Incident management (IT-01)

Owner: IT. Effective: 2025-01-01. Status: active at 2026-11-26.

## Requirements

Severity 1: business-critical outage without workaround; appoint commander within 15 minutes and conduct review within five business days. Severity 2: major degradation with partial workaround. Recovery is not final closure.

## Evidence

Record the affected entity, decision date, applicable rule, evidence and approving role. Approval in a different process does not override these requirements.
