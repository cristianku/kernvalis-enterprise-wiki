# Chicago, Illinois, USA — operating charter

Site: [CHI](../sites/CHI.md). Approved 2026-01-02. Headcount planning baseline: 150. This charter supplements rather than replaces the corporate organization record.

## Local operating model

The office or distribution/service operation coordinates customer-facing and corporate activities. Local management is accountable for site availability, personnel safety, record accuracy and escalation of process exceptions.

Local functional groups: [Logistics](../departments/LOG.md), [Sales](../departments/SAL.md), [Customer Service](../departments/SVC.md), [Finance](../departments/FIN.md), [Quality](../departments/QA.md), [IT](../departments/IT.md), [Human Resources](../departments/HR.md), [Procurement](../departments/PUR.md). Functions not represented locally are provided by their corporate or regional counterparts. The site manager cannot create exceptions to Finance, Quality, Security or Legal requirements merely because the request originates locally.

## Cadence

Daily review of operational interruptions; weekly cross-functional review of unresolved customer or production commitments; monthly headcount and budget reconciliation; quarterly continuity exercise review. Deviations require an accountable owner and an explicit expiry or closure date.

## Escalation

Immediate safety or security concerns are escalated to the relevant independent function. Financial commitments go through the corporate approval matrix. See [decision rights](../governance/decision-rights.md) and [IT incident policy](../it/incident-management.md).
