# 2006 corporate history — First standard drive family

Record ID: HIST-2006. This is an enterprise historical summary compiled for the November 2026 archive; it is not itself a policy or financial statement.

## Development

Management approved a standard motion-control product program rather than relying entirely on bespoke engineering. Product variants began to be managed by controlled part numbers, enabling repeatable manufacturing and spare-parts support.
