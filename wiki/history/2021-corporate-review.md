# 2021 corporate history — Supply constraints and lead times

Record ID: HIST-2021. This is an enterprise historical summary compiled for the November 2026 archive; it is not itself a policy or financial statement.

## Development

Supplier risk reviews were expanded after component shortages. Engineering established controlled substitution workflows rather than allowing procurement to treat equivalent-looking components as approved alternates.
