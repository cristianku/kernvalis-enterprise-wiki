# 2005 corporate history — Founding and first motion-control laboratory

Record ID: HIST-2005. This is an enterprise historical summary compiled for the November 2026 archive; it is not itself a policy or financial statement.

## Development

Kernvalis was founded in Austin to supply motion-control engineering services for industrial customers. Early work centered on custom drives and integration. The founding decision kept engineering and customer support in one location; subsequent sites are recorded in the later organizational history.
