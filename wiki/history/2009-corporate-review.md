# 2009 corporate history — Field support expansion

Record ID: HIST-2009. This is an enterprise historical summary compiled for the November 2026 archive; it is not itself a policy or financial statement.

## Development

A centralized service queue was established for installed equipment. Field technicians were required to retain serial references and original customer reports rather than relying on informal telephone notes.
