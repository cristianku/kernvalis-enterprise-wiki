# Decision record DEC-2025-014: Credit policy revision

Decision date: 2025-06-12. Effective date: 2025-07-01.

Before July 2025, the maximum overdue age for automatic clearance was 45 calendar days and unbilled shipments were excluded from exposure. The Finance steering committee approved a 30-day threshold and inclusion of unbilled shipments after reconciliation identified systematic underestimation of exposure.

The revised policy applies to orders evaluated on or after the effective date, including re-evaluations of amended orders. Earlier decisions remain valid historical records and must not be retroactively rewritten. The authoritative current policy is [FIN-CR-01](../finance/credit-policy.md).
